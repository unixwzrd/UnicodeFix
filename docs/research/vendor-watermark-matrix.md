## Evidence rules

Use primary vendor documentation, specifications, or the maintainer’s repository; retain access date, version/commit, model/tokenizer identifiers, configuration fingerprint, and synthetic fixture provenance. Do not upload user data to a vendor detector. Re-survey at each release, and preserve negative findings alongside positive ones.

## What cleanup can honestly promise

UnicodeFix can find and clean exact locally observable Unicode/control and recognized provenance carriers. It can report formatting patterns such as column wrapping without calling them AI signals. Statistical schemes are only profile-specific research results: a `not_detected` result says nothing about other schemes or configurations, and unknown/proprietary schemes have no safe generic removal path.
